Weather Within Reach
Interactive audiovisual installation
Max / AI-assisted visuals
June–August 2026

    
Inspired by a night of playing in a thunderstorm with my brother and a friend. Max turns body movement and position into changes in sound and moving image, so rain, wind, thunder, earthquake and tsunami answer the participant's actions. Tested with five participants in one-minute sessions.

    
wave your arm, summon a storm

Muddy River
Personal project / mixed-reality experience
Landscape evolution of Back Bay Fens
August–October 2026

    
An on-site mixed-reality experience that overlays dated maps, photographs and point clouds onto Back Bay Fens, so visitors can compare the past with the park in front of them. I built a working prototype for Quest in Unreal Engine.

    
the park has a secret past

    
Watch the demoyoutu.be/QymWRwJSRiE

  






  

    03
    

    
Soli Axis
Group project / sustainable product & system design
July 2026 / UNESCO Youth Education Research Centre, Art for 2030
My part: research and the final video

    
A modular solar unit for the façades of existing buildings. It mounts by vacuum suction instead of drilling, tracks the sun, folds away in strong wind and cleans itself, while an app and web platform let residents monitor output and share surplus energy.

    
unused walls → free sunlight

    
Watch the videoyoutu.be/GI8KOWjKA9g

Signal
Personal project / AI film / 7 min 09 sec
April–August 2026

    
An AI short film about prediction, belief and the cost of certainty. A detective investigates SIGNAL, a system whose promise turns users into believers. When it predicts his own future, the investigation becomes a personal choice about free will.

    
what if the machine is right about you?
